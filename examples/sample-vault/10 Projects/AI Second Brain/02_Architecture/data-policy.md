# Data policy (sample)

## 1 · Classification
| Label | Meaning | May reach a model |
|---|---|---|
| `public` | Published or publishable material | Yes |
| `internal` | The owner's own notes and captures | Yes, local model only |
| `client` | Material belonging to a client or counterparty | Never; it does not belong in the vault |

A note with no label, or a label the runtime cannot read with certainty, is refused.
Absence of a label is not permission.

## 2 · Retention
Failed answers are kept for 90 days in a local store outside the vault, then deleted.
The audit log records each step without the question or answer text.

## 3 · Third parties
No names, health or financial details of anyone other than the owner.
